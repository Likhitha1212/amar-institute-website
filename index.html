About Us

Amar's Institute of Physics is committed to helping students develop a deep understanding of Physics through conceptual learning, regular practice, and personalized guidance.

The institute is led by an experienced Physics educator with over 25 years of teaching experience. Having served at reputed educational institutions such as Narayana Educational Institutions and Sri Chaitanya Educational Institutions, he has successfully mentored hundreds of students in achieving academic excellence in Board Examinations, NEET, and JEE.

At Amar's Institute of Physics, the focus is on building strong fundamentals, improving analytical thinking, and instilling confidence in every student through a structured and student-centric approach.
